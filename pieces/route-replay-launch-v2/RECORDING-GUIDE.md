# Vertical recordings for the trailer

Record all three clips vertically at your phone's native resolution and aspect ratio. The overall trailer stays 16:9; each clip sits in a tall portrait frame without stretching or cropping. Its placement changes between scenes.

| Clip | Trailer time | Used length | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01-library.mov | 6–8s | 2s | Show the library, then tap the running workout. |
| 02-details.mov | 11–13s | 2s | Show the route and stats, then gently lower the stats card. |
| 03-replay.mov | 17–20s | 3s | Capture a smooth stretch of the loaded 3D satellite replay. |

Record 6–10 seconds per take, with a little still time before and after the action. Use the same device and appearance for all three, ideally at 30 or 60 fps. Do not add landscape padding or resize the files before supplying them.

The current placeholders reserve 432 × 936 pixels, a tall phone-shaped envelope. When the files arrive, preserve their actual portrait proportions and fit them within that envelope. Each slot's position and exact timing are saved in `recording-slots.json`.

Example mode offers a consistent library → details → replay path. For the flyover, let map imagery load and set a comfortable speed before recording. Keep the app's original map attribution visible.
