# Clean Launch — reusable custom style study

Status: approved and rendered. The user's request to render approved the revised dark palette and storyboard.

## Intent

A reusable procedural animation style distilled from the two user-selected launch films. The demonstration uses original abstract artwork: a blue idea gains form, becomes a canvas, and returns as a finished mark. No real product is being represented by the demo.

1920 × 1080, 16:9, 8 seconds at 30 fps, 240 frames. Four subframes of motion blur. 120 BPM is the demo score clock, not a measured tempo of the references. Music and effects are composed locally; no narration.

Palette revision: the user requested darker versions of the colors for a more premium feel. The current draft uses deep ink blue, muted slate/cyan, smoked violet, charcoal type and pearl surfaces. Highlights and ambient bloom are more restrained than in the first draft.

## References studied

- [Introducing ChatGPT Atlas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ej6hnsQgV_c): white title cards, floating windows, cool ambient edge light, camera moves into actual UI.
- [You can just launch Sites | ChatGPT Work](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6VwIXjFj6YQ): centered sentence-case headlines, action-word color, continuous typography and UI scale changes, oversized cursor detail.

Reference clips, derived stills, numerical measurements and comparisons are local in the ignored `references/launch-style/` folder. Their pixels are not included in the demo. The final output uses no source logos, product screens, music or footage.

## Timing and implementation

- User-directed motion revision: the opening line starts at 62 px, grows to 154 px with an overshoot from 0.20–1.15 s, then lifts away from 1.30–1.63 s. Its dot remains to become the sphere.
- Full stop and thought card arrive on springs from 1.35–2.30 s. The sphere has brief squash/stretch and a small vertical bounce. Focus copy enters on springs; the secondary line is now 70 px.
- Card changes aspect and sphere rounds into a tile from 3.35–4.40 s. Aperture opens from 3.63–4.38 s; a small damped tilt accompanies the change. Orbital hairlines finish by 4.72 s.
- Artboard contracts into the final mark from 5.60–6.62 s with a brief landing bounce. The closing sentence scales and settles by 6.82 s, then holds through frame 239.
- Score is dry before the quiet interval; the largest warm chord and low hit land at 4.0 s. The final two-note motif resolves more quietly.
- Continuous spring interpolation uses one surface and one hero. The opening music adds a rising five-note motif to support the growing title. The single-file HTML draws all graphics and synthesizes all sound; it contains no reference assets.

The abstract shape relay is a custom study, not a fully proven narrative format. Longer launch pieces should use genuine supplied product screenshots for proof and keep each movement tied to a meaningful action.

## Claims

The demo is abstract design copy and makes no factual product claims. Observations about the reference style are grounded in the supplied films and the local measurement files. Automatic cut detection is approximate and was compared with contact sheets.
