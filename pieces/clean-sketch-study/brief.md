# Clean Sketch — premium × sketchbook experiment

The user asked to render a combination of their custom launch style and the installed Sketchbook style. This trial keeps the same sequence and bouncy timing, allowing a direct comparison with `clean-launch-study` revision 2.

1920 × 1080, 16:9, eight seconds, 30 fps, 240 frames, 120 BPM. All graphics and sound are procedural. No narration, factual product claims, logos or source-film pixels.

## Combination

The darker navy/slate/smoked-violet palette, clean typography, spring motion and persistent dot/sphere/tile/mark come from the user's custom style. Warm pearl paper, graphite linework, notebook binding, hatching, construction strokes and handwritten emphasis come from Sketchbook. Movement remains smooth on every frame; pencil tremor changes on twos.

| Time | Beat | Material / motion | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Start with an idea. | Small sentence grows and bounces; handwritten `idea` is underlined by a pencil; sentence lifts away as the dot becomes a navy sphere. | Rising motif and faint pencil scratch. |
| 2–4 s | What if? | Notebook thought card with handwritten headline; sphere has graphite edges, hatching and a construction loop; an arrow draws toward it. | Warm short tones, soft scratch; quiet before the reveal. |
| 4–6 s | Make it tangible. | Deep slate surround reveals the notebook artboard; sphere rounds to a hatched tile; handwritten keyword and pencil orbits complete the thought. | Largest original warm chord at 4.0 s, then decay. |
| 6–8 s | An idea. Made real. | Artwork contracts into a small hand-outlined mark; closing line springs in with handwritten `real.`. | Quieter resolving motif. |

## Sources and scope

The custom parent is `styles/clean-launch/`; the sketchbook parent is the installed animate skill's `styles/sketchbook/`, including its inspected sample and documented helper API. The hybrid is a new merged kit with one `STYLE`, not two conflicting kits in one composition.

The original custom render remains in its original piece. This experiment has its own source, renders, review, and reusable style folder. Handwriting uses local fonts, so the exact letter shapes can vary on other machines.
